# ADR 0019 - Cross-Slice RevitDOM Scope and Engine Eviction

Status: Accepted

## Context
Original ADR 019 elevated **RevitDOM** to a top-level folder outside the legacy `Modules/` directory to break coupling between business logic and WPF presentation, while chartering **RevitDOM** to `"house all backend engines"`—including diffing, standards execution, and duplicate analysis—and leaving WPF views, ViewModels, and commands in `Modules/`.

That charter created three architectural tensions once [ADR 0028](0028-hybrid-synthetic-core-assembly-and-colocated-partitions.md) introduced the multi-targeted `Synthetic.Core` assembly (`net48;net8.0-windows8.0`) and co-located `Core/` + `Addin/` vertical slice partitions:

1. **Catch-All Shared Layer Eroding Slice Locality:** Housing single-feature orchestration engines (`RevitDOM/Operations/Standards/*`, `RevitDOM/Operations/Merge/MergeAnalysisEngine.cs`, and `RevitDOM/Utilities/NamingUtils.cs`) inside **RevitDOM** turned a cross-slice BIM infrastructure layer into a catch-all separated from each feature's commands, ViewModels, and views.
2. **Compiler & Dependency-Direction Friction:** Under [ADR 0028](0028-hybrid-synthetic-core-assembly-and-colocated-partitions.md), pure `Core/` code must compile in `Synthetic.Core` with zero `Autodesk.Revit.*` references, zero `.xaml`, and zero `#if REVIT202*` directives, while **RevitDOM** must remain strictly downstream-agnostic (`Features` -> **RevitDOM** -> `Platform`). Leaving feature orchestration inside **RevitDOM** blocked clean `Core/`/`Addin/` splitting and risked pulling feature UI dependencies into shared BIM infrastructure.
3. **Cross-Slice Shared Diff & Duplicate POCOs:** Diff and duplicate-review POCO models ([`DuplicateClusterModel`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/DuplicateClusterModel.cs), [`DuplicateItemModel`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/DuplicateItemModel.cs), [`DuplicateTypeModel`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/DuplicateTypeModel.cs), [`TypeMappingModel`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/TypeMappingModel.cs), [`ParameterDiffRowModel`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/ParameterDiffRowModel.cs), and [`RecommendedAction`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/RecommendedAction.cs)) are consumed jointly by both **Duplicate Merging** and **Standards Dashboard**, requiring a shared cross-slice home rather than placement inside a single feature slice.

## Decision
We supersede the original ADR 019 `"house all backend engines"` clause and `Modules/` framing, partition **RevitDOM** into [`src/RevitDOM/Core/`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/) (`Synthetic.Core`) and [`src/RevitDOM/Addin/`](../../src/RevitDOM/Addin/) (`Synthetic<Year>`), lock **RevitDOM** strictly to cross-slice BIM infrastructure, and evict single-slice engines into their co-located vertical feature slices:

1. **Supersede `"house all backend engines"` and `Modules/` Framing:**
   - **RevitDOM** no longer houses feature-specific orchestration engines, workflows, or report generators, and feature presentation is no longer framed around `Modules/`.
   - Each vertical feature owns its pure domain modules (`Core/`), Revit adapters (`Addin/`), commands, ViewModels, and WPF views under `src/Features/<Slice>/{Core, Addin}/` per [ADR 0027](0027-tiered-vertical-slice-architecture-and-seam-cqs.md) and [ADR 0028](0028-hybrid-synthetic-core-assembly-and-colocated-partitions.md).

2. **Cross-Slice RevitDOM Scope ([`src/RevitDOM/Core/`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/) + [`src/RevitDOM/Addin/`](../../src/RevitDOM/Addin/)):**
   - **Pure Cross-Slice BIM Infrastructure (`src/RevitDOM/Core/`, compiled into `Synthetic.Core`):**
     - Pure POCO domain models in [`src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/), including the cross-slice diff and clustering models shared by **Duplicate Merging** and **Standards Dashboard**: [`DuplicateClusterModel`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/DuplicateClusterModel.cs), [`DuplicateItemModel`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/DuplicateItemModel.cs), [`DuplicateTypeModel`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/DuplicateTypeModel.cs), [`TypeMappingModel`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/TypeMappingModel.cs), [`ParameterDiffRowModel`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/ParameterDiffRowModel.cs), and [`RecommendedAction`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/RecommendedAction.cs).
     - Pure type metadata registry: [`StandardTypeMetadataRegistry`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Operations/StandardTypeMetadataRegistry.cs) (`BucketName`, topological tier, **Document Scope**, composite key selectors, and **Source Pane** taxonomy).
     - Pure **Serialization Engine** JSON serializer and polymorphic hydrator: [`PocoJsonSerializer`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Operations/PocoJsonSerializer.cs).
     - Pure **Identity Comparison** module: [`PocoIdentityService`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Translation/PocoIdentityService.cs) and [`IPocoIdentityService`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Translation/IPocoIdentityService.cs).
     - Pure **Deep Diff Comparison** contract: [`IDiffEngine`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Operations/Diffing/IDiffEngine.cs).
     - Pure topological ordering and POCO dependency scanning: [`ImportExecutionRunner`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Operations/ImportExecutionRunner.cs) and [`RevitDomDependencyScanner`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Operations/RevitDomDependencyScanner.cs).
   - **Revit-Connected BIM Infrastructure (`src/RevitDOM/Addin/`, compiled into `Synthetic<Year>`):**
     - [`StandardTypeRegistry`](../../src/RevitDOM/Addin/Operations/StandardTypeRegistry.cs), composing [`StandardTypeMetadataRegistry`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Operations/StandardTypeMetadataRegistry.cs) with live `Autodesk.Revit.DB` types, translator bindings, and root collectors (refining [ADR 0024](0024-unified-internal-standardtyperegistry-and-composite-class-routing.md)).
     - Translators and dispatchers in [`src/RevitDOM/Addin/Translation/`](../../src/RevitDOM/Addin/Translation/).
     - Revit-connected **Serialization Engine**: [`StandardSerializationEngine`](../../src/RevitDOM/Addin/Operations/StandardSerializationEngine.cs) and [`IStandardSerializationEngine`](../../src/RevitDOM/Addin/Operations/IStandardSerializationEngine.cs).
     - Revit-connected **Identity Comparison**: [`RevitIdentityService`](../../src/RevitDOM/Addin/Translation/RevitIdentityService.cs) and [`IIdentityService`](../../src/RevitDOM/Addin/Translation/IIdentityService.cs).
     - Revit-connected **Deep Diff Comparison**: [`PocoToRevitDiffEngine`](../../src/RevitDOM/Addin/Operations/Diffing/PocoToRevitDiffEngine.cs).
     - **Alias Swapping** and element/parameter helpers: [`AliasSwapEngine`](../../src/RevitDOM/Addin/Operations/AliasSwapEngine.cs) (per [ADR 0022](0022-unified-6-phase-alias-swap-and-reference-redirection-engine-architecture.md)), [`GraphicOverrideUtility`](../../src/RevitDOM/Addin/Operations/GraphicOverrideUtility.cs), [`MaterialAssetEngine`](../../src/RevitDOM/Addin/Operations/MaterialAssetEngine.cs), and [`ParameterEngine`](../../src/RevitDOM/Addin/Operations/ParameterEngine.cs).

3. **Eviction of `19` Standards Orchestration Modules to [`src/Features/StandardsManagement/`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/):**
   - **Pure `Core/` ([`src/Features/StandardsManagement/Core/`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Core/)):** Evicts the `9` pure standards orchestration modules from `RevitDOM/Operations/Standards/`—[`FindReplaceService`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Core/FindReplaceService.cs) / [`IFindReplaceService`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Core/IFindReplaceService.cs), [`PathResolutionUtility`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Core/PathResolutionUtility.cs), [`StandardsMergeUtility`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Core/StandardsMergeUtility.cs), [`StandardsReportGenerator`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Core/StandardsReportGenerator.cs), and execution/log DTOs ([`ImportLogItem`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Core/ImportLogItem.cs), [`StandardsExecutionItem`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Core/StandardsExecutionItem.cs), [`StandardsExecutionOptions`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Core/StandardsExecutionOptions.cs), [`StandardsExecutionResult`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Core/StandardsExecutionResult.cs))—alongside summary-display abstractions ([`ISummaryDisplayService`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Core/ISummaryDisplayService.cs), [`NoOpSummaryDisplayService`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Core/NoOpSummaryDisplayService.cs)).
   - **Revit-Connected `Addin/` ([`src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/)):** Evicts the `10` Revit-coupled standards orchestration modules from `RevitDOM/Operations/Standards/`—**Standards Execution Pipeline** ([`StandardsExecutionPipeline`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/StandardsExecutionPipeline.cs) / [`IStandardsExecutionPipeline`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/IStandardsExecutionPipeline.cs)), **Family Enforcer** ([`RevitFamilyEnforcer`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/RevitFamilyEnforcer.cs) / [`IFamilyEnforcer`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/IFamilyEnforcer.cs)), [`StandardsExtractionOrchestrator`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/StandardsExtractionOrchestrator.cs) / [`IStandardsExtractionOrchestrator`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/IStandardsExtractionOrchestrator.cs), [`StandardsDiffEngine`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/StandardsDiffEngine.cs), [`StandardsExportService`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/StandardsExportService.cs) / [`IStandardsExportService`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/IStandardsExportService.cs), and [`StandardsHierarchyUtility`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/StandardsHierarchyUtility.cs)—co-locating them with [`WindowsSummaryDisplayService`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/WindowsSummaryDisplayService.cs) and the **Standards Dashboard** [`Commands/`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/Commands/), [`Settings/`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/Settings/), [`ViewModels/`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/ViewModels/), and [`Views/`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/Views/).

4. **Eviction of Duplicate-Merge Engines to [`src/Features/MergeDuplicates/`](../../src/Features/MergeDuplicates/):**
   - **Pure `Core/` ([`src/Features/MergeDuplicates/Core/`](../../src/Features/MergeDuplicates/Core/)):** Evicts [`MergeAnalysisEngine`](../../src/Features/MergeDuplicates/Core/Services/MergeAnalysisEngine.cs) and [`NamingUtils`](../../src/Features/MergeDuplicates/Core/Utilities/NamingUtils.cs) out of **RevitDOM**, completing the pure-POCO engine separation initiated in [ADR 0021](0021-decouple-mergeanalysisengine-into-pure-poco-engine-and-duplicate-candidate-harvesting.md).
   - **Revit-Connected `Addin/` ([`src/Features/MergeDuplicates/Addin/`](../../src/Features/MergeDuplicates/Addin/)):** Co-locates [`CmdMergeDuplicates`](../../src/Features/MergeDuplicates/Addin/Commands/CmdMergeDuplicates.cs), [`ProcessMergeEventHandler`](../../src/Features/MergeDuplicates/Addin/Handlers/ProcessMergeEventHandler.cs), **Duplicate Candidate Harvesting** ([`RevitMergeDataCollector`](../../src/Features/MergeDuplicates/Addin/Services/RevitMergeDataCollector.cs)), [`Views/`](../../src/Features/MergeDuplicates/Addin/Views/), and [`ViewModels/`](../../src/Features/MergeDuplicates/Addin/ViewModels/).

5. **Automated Boundary & Test Enforcement:**
   - [`ArchitectureFitnessTests.cs`](../../tests/Architecture/Logic/ArchitectureFitnessTests.cs) (`FitnessRule.CoLocatedPartitionLayout`, `FitnessRule.CorePurityAndZeroPreprocessor`, and `FitnessRule.FourBandDependencyGraph` in [`ArchitectureFitnessRules.cs`](../../tests/Architecture/Logic/ArchitectureFitnessRules.cs), per [ADR 0029](0029-isomorphic-test-mirror-and-architecture-fitness-guardrails.md)) asserts that the retired `SyntheticShared` root and legacy `RevitDOM/Operations/{Standards, Merge}` directories do not exist, blocks `Synthetic.Features`, `Synthetic.Modules`, `Synthetic.Shared`, `Synthetic.Platform.UI`, `System.Windows`, and `Autodesk.Revit.UI` imports across **RevitDOM**, blocks sibling feature imports across `Features/<Slice>`, and enforces `Autodesk.Revit.*`-free multi-TFM compilation for `Core/` and [`tests/RevitDOM/Logic/`](../../tests/RevitDOM/Logic/).
   - Headless **Logic Tests** for evicted pure logic reside alongside each slice under [`tests/Features/StandardsManagement/Logic/`](../../tests/Features/StandardsManagement/Logic/) and [`tests/Features/MergeDuplicates/Logic/`](../../tests/Features/MergeDuplicates/Logic/).

## Alternatives Considered
1. **Retain All Backend Engines Inside RevitDOM:** Rejected because keeping single-slice **Standards Execution Pipeline**, **Family Enforcer**, and `MergeAnalysisEngine` inside **RevitDOM** violates vertical slice locality, couples cross-slice BIM infrastructure to feature-specific orchestration, and breaks one-way dependency enforcement.
2. **Move Shared Diff & Duplicate Models (`DuplicateClusterModel`, `ParameterDiffRowModel`, etc.) into `Features/MergeDuplicates/Core/`:** Rejected because **Standards Dashboard** ([`StandardsDiffEngine`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/StandardsDiffEngine.cs), [`StandardsReviewViewModel`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/ViewModels/StandardsReviewViewModel.cs), [`StagingQueueViewModel`](../../src/Features/StandardsManagement/Addin/ViewModels/StagingQueueViewModel.cs)) depends on these same POCO types; relocating them inside `MergeDuplicates` would introduce forbidden sibling-feature imports.

## Consequences
* **Pros:** Locks **RevitDOM** to reusable cross-slice BIM models, registries, translators, **Serialization Engine**, **Identity Comparison**, **Deep Diff Comparison**, and **Alias Swapping**; restores vertical slice co-location for **Standards Dashboard** / **Standards Execution Pipeline** and **Duplicate Merging**; and enforces strict compiler and **Architecture Fitness Tests** guarantees across `Synthetic.Core` (`net48;net8.0-windows8.0`) and `Synthetic<Year>`.
* **Cons:** Retains `"Duplicate*"`-prefixed POCO types inside [`src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/`](../../src/RevitDOM/Core/Models/) as cross-slice diff/review contracts; splits metadata/contract seams (`StandardTypeMetadataRegistry` vs. `StandardTypeRegistry`, `PocoJsonSerializer` vs. `StandardSerializationEngine`, `PocoIdentityService` vs. `RevitIdentityService`, `IDiffEngine` vs. `PocoToRevitDiffEngine`) across `Core/` and `Addin/`; and shifts legacy `Synthetic.RevitDOM.Operations.Standards` callers to `Synthetic.Features.StandardsManagement`.
